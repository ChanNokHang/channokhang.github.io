## Research interests

My research focuses on systemic risk, financial networks, graphical models, and machine learning applications in finance. I am particularly interested in how relationships among stocks can help us understand market dynamics.

<article class="research-entry" markdown="1">
<p class="eyebrow">Master's thesis · NYCU · 2025</p>

### Mapping Risk Contagion through Graphical Models and Early Warning Signals in Taiwan’s Stock Market

**Research question.** How can stock interconnections help us measure systemic risk and examine potential early warning signals in Taiwan's stock market?

**Approach.** The research uses Granger causality and functional structural equation models (FSEM) to represent relationships among stocks as financial networks.

**Focus.** Network structure and measures of interconnectedness provide a way to study market dynamics alongside traditional market-level information.

**Adviser:** [Prof. Huei-Wen Teng](https://venteng.github.io/)
